♠️ Spades

The classic partnership trick-taking game. Two teams of two — partners sit across from each other.

  1. It must be exactly 2 v 2 to start — drag players between Team Red and Team Blue, or add CPUs to fill open seats.
  2. Everyone is dealt 13 cards, face down on their phone until they choose to look.
  3. Each player bids in turn how many tricks they'll take, or Nil for none. Bid Blind Nil before looking for double stakes.
  4. Follow the led suit if you can; otherwise play anything. Spades are always trump.
  5. You can't lead a spade until spades are broken — unless spades are all you hold.
  6. Make your team's combined bid: +10 per trick bid and +1 per extra trick (a bag). Miss it and lose 10 per trick bid.
  7. Nil is ±100 and Blind Nil is ±200, scored on its own. Tricks a nil bidder takes don't help the bid — they're bags.
  8. Every 10 bags costs 100 points.
  9. First team to the target score wins — a team that falls to −200 loses.
