♥️ Hearts

The classic four-player trick-taking game. Points are bad — the lowest score wins.

  1. Exactly four players sit at the table — add CPUs to fill empty seats.
  2. Each hand starts by passing 3 cards to another player. The direction rotates clockwise, counter-clockwise, across, then a hold hand with no passing.
  3. The player holding the 2♣ leads the first trick. Follow the led suit if you can; otherwise play anything.
  4. Highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads the next one.
  5. Every ♥ is +1, the Q♠ is +13, and the J♦ is −10.
  6. You can't lead hearts until they've been broken — unless hearts are all you hold.
  7. Shoot the moon: take all 13 hearts and the Q♠ and you score 0 while everyone else takes 26.
  8. When anyone reaches the target score the game ends, and the lowest score wins.
