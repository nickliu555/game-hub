🥊 Ragdoll Rumble

A wobbly ragdoll brawl. The fight plays out on this screen while each player steers their ragdoll from their phone.

  1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone (2–6 fighters)
  2. Short on players? Hit 🤖 Add CPU and pick a difficulty. Drag the list to change colors and spawn spots
  3. Set rounds to win, round length, items and the stage set, then hit Start!
  4. On their phones: the thumbstick moves and aims, JUMP jumps (again in the air for a double and a triple jump) and ACTION does everything else
  5. Tap ACTION to punch (three quick taps make a combo; aim down to kick); hold ACTION to charge a smash and let go to launch it — it fires the way you started charging, and a full charge holds for about a second before it fires itself
  6. In the air, stomp straight down onto someone below you — landing it with the tip of your foot — to spike them — off the edge it's usually a KO
  7. Tap ACTION next to someone reeling from a hit (not a plain jab or a throw) or who just swung a smash to grab them (dizzy stars spin over their head and their eyes turn to spirals), then tap again to throw them forward, back (strongest), up, or hold down to slam them. Grabbed? Mash ACTION to break free
  8. Every hit raises your damage % — the higher it is, the further you fly. Hold the stick while flying to steer back. Stuck against the side of the stage? Hold into the wall to cling, then press JUMP to wall-kick back up
  9. Items pop up around the stage: 🏏 bat, 💣 bomb and 🪨 rock (tap ACTION to swing or throw) and a 🥊 glove that powers up your punches (no other items while they're on). Get knocked limp or grabbed and you drop it, gloves included
  10. Press the stick down to drop through a floating platform — on solid ground it ducks under straight punches, bat swings and flat throws (down kicks and smashes still land). Each stage has its own twist — watch out!
  11. Fly past the edge of the screen and you're out. When the clock runs out, sudden death closes the edges in. Last ragdoll standing wins the round — first to the target round wins takes the game
