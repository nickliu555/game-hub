    🏐 Blob Ball

    Bouncy blob volleyball on this screen, with each player steering a blob from their phone.

    1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone — it's 1v1
    2. No opponent? Hit 🤖 Add CPU to play against the computer
    3. Drag a player onto the other one to swap who plays left and right
    4. Pick the points to win, then hit Play
    5. On the phone: ◀ ▶ buttons on the left to move (the ⚙️ gear switches to a joystick), JUMP on the right — hold for a full jump, tap for a short hop
    6. A Bluetooth controller paired with the phone works too: left stick or D-pad moves, A jumps
    7. You can only move on your own side of the net
    8. Knock the ball with your blob as many times as you like — where it hits your dome sets its direction, and moving or jumping into it hits it harder
    9. The ball bounces off the walls, the ceiling and the net
    10. Land the ball on your opponent's sand to score — whoever lost the point serves next
    11. First to the target score wins! Then hit ▶ Play again for a rematch, or go back to the lobby
