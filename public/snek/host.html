🐍 Snek

Snek plays out on this screen while each player steers their snake from their phone.

  1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone (1–4 players). Hit 🤖 Add CPU to add computer snakes
  2. A single snake (one player, or a lone CPU) plays classic solo Snake: one apple at a time, one life — grow the longest snake to beat the session best
  3. With 2–4 snakes, set the round length, how many round wins take the game and Power-ups On/Off, then hit Start!
  4. On their phones: joystick, swipe or tap zones steer up / down / left / right
  5. Eat apples to grow
  6. Hit the edge of the map, a wall, yourself or another snake and you're out for the round
  7. Every so often a rare golden apple worth +3 length appears on the map
  8. Head-on collisions take out both snakes, and everyone speeds up as the round goes on
  9. Power-ups (multiplayer, when On): 🧲 Magnet pulls apples and golden apples within 3 cells to you for 6s; 👻 Phantom gives 1.2× speed and lets you pass through walls and snakes for 6s — but the edges still kill
  10. The longest snake wins the round, not the last one standing. A crashed snake keeps the length it crashed at (it just stops growing), and on a tie a snake that's still alive wins
  11. The round ends when time runs out, when everyone has crashed, or once a lone survivor is already longer than every crashed snake
  12. First to the target number of round wins takes the game!
