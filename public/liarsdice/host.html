🎲 Liar's Dice

The pirate's bluffing game. The last player with dice wins.

  1. 2 to 8 players — add CPUs to fill seats. Everyone starts with 3, 4, 5 or 6 dice (host's choice).
  2. Each round everyone rolls in secret. Your dice show only on your phone — tap them to hide them from wandering eyes.
  3. Players take turns bidding on how many dice of one face are on the whole table, counting everyone's dice — e.g. "five 4s".
  4. 1s are wild and count as every other face.
  5. Each bid must go up: more dice, or the same number of a higher face.
  6. Bidding on 1s needs only half as many (rounded up). Switching back off 1s needs double plus one.
  7. Instead of raising, call BS: every die is revealed. Fewer than bid? The bidder loses a die. Otherwise, you do.
  8. Or call Spot On: if the count is exactly right you win a die back (up to your starting count); otherwise you lose one.
  9. Whoever lost a die opens the next round. Lose all your dice and you're out — the last player with dice wins!
