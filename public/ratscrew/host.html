🐀 Egyptian Ratscrew

Flip, watch the pile, and slap it first. The last player holding cards wins.

  1. 2 to 8 players — add CPUs to fill seats. The whole deck is dealt out face down.
  2. On your turn, tap the bottom half of your phone to flip your top card onto the pile. Every card stays on the pile for at least 2 seconds before the next one can follow.
  3. Tap SLAP on the top half of your phone when the pile shows a Double (two of the same rank in a row), a Sandwich (same rank with one card between) or, if it's on, a Flush (three of the same suit in a row). The first slap takes the whole pile and leads next.
  4. Slap when it isn't valid and you pay 2 cards to the player whose card you slapped. Slap your own card and they go to the next player.
  5. A J, Q, K or A gives the next player 1, 2, 3 or 4 tries to play a face card. If they can't, whoever played the face card takes the pile — unless someone slaps it first!
  6. Run out of cards and you're out. The last player holding cards wins!
