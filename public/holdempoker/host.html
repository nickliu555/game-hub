♣️ Hold'em Poker

No-Limit Texas Hold'em, tournament style. Last player with chips wins.

  1. 2 to 8 players — add CPUs to fill seats. Everyone starts with 1,500 chips.
  2. The blinds start at 10 / 20 and go up every 2, 3, 5 or 7 hands (host's choice).
  3. Each player gets 2 private cards on their phone. Five shared cards are dealt face up on the table: the flop (3), the turn (1) and the river (1).
  4. On your turn: fold, check, call, or bet / raise any amount up to your whole stack. A raise must at least double the current bet.
  5. Make the best five-card hand from your two cards and the five on the table. Best hand at showdown wins the pot.
  6. If you're all-in for less, you can only win what you matched from each player — the rest goes to a side pot.
  7. Lose all your chips and you're out. The last player standing wins the tournament!
